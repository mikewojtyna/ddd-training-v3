# Exercise 4: Archetypes Funnel on PLM

**Module:** M6 Domain exploration & archetypes · **Time:** 40 min · **Form:** pairs · **Start:** tag `ex04/start`

## Situation

Corvane Drive Systems (fictional) builds configurable actuator units for machine makers and sells simulation studies on the side. Its head of engineering has described how the business works, in the business's own words. Nobody there talks about archetypes. Your job is to find them, with the AI as a sparring partner that asks the Funnel questions but doesn't answer them.

Your materials:

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `handouts/plm-narrative.md` | the business narrative (about 1.5 pages) |
| `handouts/funnel-questions-prompt.md` | the Funnel questions for you, and the prompt card for the AI |

## Your task

1. **Read (7 min).** Both of you read the narrative. Mark the sentences that sound like a rule ("must", "never", "only", "needs").
2. **Run the Funnel (20 min).** Paste the prompt card and the narrative into the AI. Answer its questions together, one at a time. Propose archetypes yourselves; the AI must not name them first.
3. **Write the table (8 min).** Ask the AI for the "summary" and correct it. One row per archetype you found:

   | Area | Archetype | Two supporting sentences | A rule it doesn't explain |
   |---|---|---|---|

   Add a short list of what is **not** an archetype in the narrative, and why.
4. **Read the AI's additions** after the summary. Accept or reject each one with a reason.

**Extension (5 min, if you're done early):** the section *Selling analysis*. Which archetype fits it? Which of its rules is specific to Corvane and wouldn't be in a generic price list?

## Done when

- Your table has at least four archetypes, each with two quoted sentences from the narrative.
- Each row names one rule the archetype doesn't explain.
- You've listed at least two things in the narrative that are not archetypes.
- You can name one moment where the AI tried to answer instead of asking.

## Debrief questions

- Which archetype did you find first, and which sentence gave it away? Was that sentence a rule or just a word?
- Where does the same company or plant appear in several roles? What would happen if each tool kept its own copy?
- The two BOMs in engineering and in Brno: one thing or two? What does the archetype say?
- Is the phone approval of urgent changes part of the archetype, or a Corvane-specific exception?
- Did the AI keep to "ask, don't answer"? What did you learn when it didn't?
- Compare with the reference: `git show ex04/solution:docs/archetypes/plm-engineering-analysis.md`.

## Rules

- You propose the archetypes. The AI asks.
- Every archetype needs evidence from the narrative, not from your general knowledge of PLM.
