**Open questions** (I recorded your rules as you stated them and didn't sharpen them)
1. "Cap it at the available balance": if an investor asks for more than is available, is the withdrawal refused or is only the available amount paid out? The current code refuses.
2. What counts as "available"? Does it exclude money committed to investments, deposits still waiting for payment confirmation, and withdrawals that are requested but not yet paid out?
3. What happens if the investor has no verified bank account? Can they have more than one, and if so, which one gets the money?
4. Are there exceptions to "at any time", or minimum or maximum amounts, or fees?
5. Is the payout immediate or does it go through a processing period? And can the account currency differ from the bank account's?
6. What name do you want for the activity? I called it "Take money out".
