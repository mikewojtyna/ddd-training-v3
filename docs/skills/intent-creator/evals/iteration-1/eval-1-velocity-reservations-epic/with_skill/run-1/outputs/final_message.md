**How I split it**
- "Reservations" covers more than one need, so I split it by actor and by outcome. Riders and technicians reserve for different reasons (to ride, or to repair), and the bike ends up differently. When a rider's reservation expires, the bike goes back to everyone. When a technician's expires, the bike stays broken and hidden from riders. So each actor gets a "reserve" intent and an "expiry" intent.
- I didn't give "reservation released when the unlock fails" its own file. It only matters as part of unlocking, so it's listed in the *Out* scope of the unlock candidates below.
