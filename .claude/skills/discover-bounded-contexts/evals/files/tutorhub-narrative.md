## Things people said during the session
- "Payouts happen every Monday, no matter what happens with lessons."
- "Reviews can come in up to 14 days after the lesson."
- "Complaints can be filed anytime, even weeks later."
- "We use Stripe for cards and payouts, and a third-party video provider for the rooms."
