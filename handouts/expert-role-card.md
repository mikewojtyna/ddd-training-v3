# Role card: the domain expert

You are a senior product manager at VeloCity. You've been part of the design from the start. Your source is
`docs/domains/velocity/velocity-vision.md`. The modellers don't see it.

## How to play

- **Answer only from the text.** Use the language of the business: rides, bikes, fees, debt. No classes, tables or
  patterns.
- **When the text is silent, decide.** Give a plausible business answer, as a real product manager would.
  Then write the decision down (see below).
- **When the text contradicts itself, pick one reading** and write that down too. Answer with confidence. Real
  experts rarely say "our document contradicts itself".
- **Don't volunteer contradictions or gaps.** The modellers have to find them by asking. If they ask the right
  question, answer it honestly.
- **Stay consistent.** Once you've decided something, stick to it for the rest of the session.
- **It's fine to say "I don't know, I'd have to ask Finance"** for a number the text doesn't give, such as a price.
  Then decide one anyway if the modellers need it to continue.

## Your decision log

Keep a short list while you play. One line per decision:

| # | Question asked | What the vision says | What I decided |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | | silent / contradicts itself / unclear | |

You'll need it in step 5 and in the debrief.

## What to read

Read the *Background* section first. Then read these sections carefully, because they matter for Returns + Billing:

| Section | Why |
|---|---|
| *Billing* | billing phases, total fee, maximum rental time, penalty |
| *Settling the Ride* | charging, retries, outstanding debt |
| *Returns* (*Station Return*, *Drop Zone Return*) | the two ways a ride ends, lock failures, return fees |
| *Account Blocks & Debt Recovery* | what happens after a charge finally fails |
| *Key Business Rules to Know* | the rules the business repeats most |
| *Domain Vocabulary* | the terms; check them against the sections, they don't always agree |

Skim the rest (*User Onboarding*, *Renting a Bike*, *Broken Bikes & Maintenance*, *Customer Support & Incidents*).
The modellers may ask how a ride starts or what happens to a broken bike after a return.
